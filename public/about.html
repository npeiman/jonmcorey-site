About Rev. Corey

Rev. Jon M. Corey, PhD

Rev. Corey is an experienced, ordained pastor whose life of service spans a wide array of circumstances and individuals in the military, the community, and the ministry. He brings warmth, discretion, and deep professional insight to every family, group, and individual he serves.

  • Combat-decorated veteran of the Vietnam War
  • Ordained clergy by the National Association of Christian Ministers
  • PhD — former university psychology professor, dean, and counselor
  • Founder of the Professional Advisory Group, Ltd
  • Supported by well-qualified, experienced, ordained specialists

Emergency availability as well as a complimentary planning session are offered, as are non-traditional and compassionate pastoral options — depending on needs, expectations, and circumstances.
