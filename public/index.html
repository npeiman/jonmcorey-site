About Rev. Corey

Rev. Jon M. Corey, PhD

Rev. Corey is an experienced, ordained pastor whose life of service spans the military, the academy, and the ministry. He brings warmth, discretion, and deep professional insight to every family and individual he serves.

  • Combat-decorated veteran of the Vietnam War
  • Ordained clergy by the National Association of Christian Ministers
  • PhD — former university psychology professor and counselor
  • Founder of the Professional Advisory Group
  • Supported by well-qualified, experienced, ordained specialists

Emergency availability and a complimentary planning session are offered, as are non-traditional pastoral options — depending on your needs and circumstances.
